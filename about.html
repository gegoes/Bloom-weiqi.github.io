The idea behind Soft Weiqi

More room
to think.

Go is a game of patience, attention, and possibility. We built Soft Weiqi to make space for all three.

Less noise.

No rankings shouting for your attention. No crowded dashboards. Just a clear board, a thoughtful opponent, and the next move.

02

More practice.

Save your games, revisit your decisions, and notice the patterns that only appear when you slow down.

03

A kinder table.

We believe learning is better when people feel welcome. Soft Weiqi is made for beginners, teachers, and lifelong players.
